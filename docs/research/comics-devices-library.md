# Comics Devices Library: Device Cards on One Page


## Panels, Layout and Sequence

### 1. [Rhythm](https://comicsdevices.com/rhythm/)
Comics have visual rhythm and language-based rhythm. Visual rhythm guides aesthetic harmony and flow. Language-based rhythm guides pacing and expectation. Both are capable of producing evocation as an outcome.
![Rhythm](comics-devices-library-pics/Rhythm.png)

### 2. [Evocation](https://comicsdevices.com/evocation/)
The act of recalling or creating emotional or sensual effects using panel arrangement, mark-making and colour.
![Evocation](comics-devices-library-pics/Evocation.png)

### 3. [Contraction](https://comicsdevices.com/contraction/)
A successive sequence of panels or gutters that contract in size, to quicken pacing or evoke a specific effect.
![Contraction](comics-devices-library-pics/Contract.png)

### 4. [Expansion](https://comicsdevices.com/expansion/)
A successive sequence of panels or gutters that expand in size, to slow down pacing or evoke a specific effect.
![Expansion](comics-devices-library-pics/Expand.png)

### 5. [Under and Overlapping Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/under-and-overlapping-panel/)
A panel underlaps or overlaps with another's, suggesting spatial and temporal simultaneity with each other.
![Under and Overlapping Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Underoverlap.png)

### 6. [Flow](https://comicsdevices.com/flow/)
Flow arises when some or all visuals elements of the page are used to establish the best reading order as intended by the creator.
![Flow](comics-devices-library-pics/Flow.png)

### 7. [Tracking](https://comicsdevices.com/tracking/)
The reader's eye follows or 'tracks' the movement of a character or object across the panel or page. Often employs kinesthetic evocation.
![Tracking](comics-devices-library-pics/Tracking.png)

### 8. [Long Drop](https://comicsdevices.com/long-drop/)
A specific type of movement tracking that spreads vertically across more than half of the height of its container (panel, page or webpage).
![Long Drop](comics-devices-library-pics/LongDrop.png)

### 9. [Long Path](https://comicsdevices.com/long-path/)
A specific type of movement tracking that spreads horizontally across more than half of the width of its container (panel, page or webpage).
![Long Path](comics-devices-library-pics/LongPath.png)

### 10. [Smear](https://comicsdevices.com/smear/)
Partial duplication or smearing of an element to evoke motion blur or quick movement within a panel.
![Smear](comics-devices-library-pics/Smear.png)

### 11. [Pop Out](https://comicsdevices.com/pop-out/)
An element (character, object, speech balloon, etc) 'pops out' of the panel.
![Pop Out](comics-devices-library-pics/Popout.png)

### 12. [Crossing](https://comicsdevices.com/crossing/)
An element (object, character, speech balloon, etc) crosses a gutter from one panel to another. There are three types: crossing over, under and within.
![Crossing](comics-devices-library-pics/Crossing.png)

### 13. [Master View](https://comicsdevices.com/master-view/)
An arrangement of panels and gutters which utilises all or two of these devices (crossing of a partial or fully continuous background, tracking of a character and flow) to produce a scene.
![Master View](comics-devices-library-pics/MasterView.png)

### 14. [Book Gutter Crossing](https://comicsdevices.com/book-gutter-crossing/)
A long, horizontal panel or element that crosses over the gutter of a book (the ditch where left and right pages meet at the spine).
![Book Gutter Crossing](comics-devices-library-pics/BookCross.png)

### 15. [Forepanel, Backpanel and Inset](https://comicsdevices.com/forepanel-backpanel-inset/)
A panel that is placed above (forepanel) or behind (backpanel) other panels.
![Forepanel, Backpanel and Inset](comics-devices-library-pics/Backfore.png)

### 16. [Full and Partial Bleed](https://comicsdevices.com/bleed/)
A panel that takes up all four directions of the entire canvas, bleeding into the edges.
![Full and Partial Bleed](comics-devices-library-pics/Fullbleed.png)

### 17. [Repoussoir](https://comicsdevices.com/repoussoir/)
An element in the left or right foreground of a panel or the left or right side of a canvas that directs the reader's eye into the composition.
![Repoussoir](comics-devices-library-pics/Repou.png)

### 18. [Epizeuxis](https://comicsdevices.com/epizeuxis/)
Repetition of a panel that is continuous.
![Epizeuxis](comics-devices-library-pics/Epizeuxis.png)

### 19. [Diacope](https://comicsdevices.com/diacope/)
Repetition of a panel that is broken up by one or a small number of panels.
![Diacope](comics-devices-library-pics/Diacope.png)

### 20. [Epanalepsis](https://comicsdevices.com/epanalepsis/)
A sequence begins with one image and ends with an exact or almost similar image.
![Epanalepsis](comics-devices-library-pics/Epana.png)

### 21. [Harmonious Juxtaposition](https://comicsdevices.com/harmonious-juxtaposition/)
A set, series or pattern of related imageries that connects the preceding and succeeding panels harmoniously within the narrative or context of a page/screen layout.
![Harmonious Juxtaposition](comics-devices-library-pics/Harmo.png)

### 22. [Discordant Juxtaposition](https://comicsdevices.com/discordant-juxtaposition/)
A set, series or pattern of unrelated, sometimes random, imageries that does not connect the preceding and succeeding panels within the narrative or context of a page/screen layout.
![Discordant Juxtaposition](comics-devices-library-pics/Discordant.png)

### 23. [Polyptych](https://comicsdevices.com/polyptych/)
Multiple events occuring in temporal or spatial simultaneity are grouped or sectioned into respective sets of panels. May include a dominant panel or forepanel that anchors the events in question.
![Polyptych](comics-devices-library-pics/Polyptych.png)

### 24. [Call and Response](https://comicsdevices.com/call-and-response/)
A sequence in which the first panel calls and the final panel responds directly to it, separated by a barrier: a panel, series of panels or a book gutter.
![Call and Response](comics-devices-library-pics/CallRespo.png)

### 25. [Cross-cutting](https://comicsdevices.com/cross-cutting/)
Alternating panels juxtaposed together to establish continuity of actions within a scene occuring at different places, or scenes occuring at a different place and/or time.
![Cross-cutting](comics-devices-library-pics/Crosscut.png)

### 26. [Recto-verso parallel](https://comicsdevices.com/recto-verso-parallel/)
The right (recto) and left (verso) sides of a two-page layout are utilised for cross-cutting and call and response.
![Recto-verso parallel](comics-devices-library-pics/Rectoverso.png)

### 27. [Match-cutting](https://comicsdevices.com/match-cutting/)
The composition of a panel is maintained as it transitions to another scene.
![Match-cutting](comics-devices-library-pics/Matchcut.png)

### 28. [Large-Small Panels](https://comicsdevices.com/large-small-panels/)
A panel placed together with relatively large or small panel(s) to explicitly feature contrast, perspective or character dynamics.
![Large-Small Panels](comics-devices-library-pics/Largesmall.png)

### 29. [Establishing Shot](https://comicsdevices.com/establishing-shot/)
A panel of any size with the sole visual-narrative purpose of establishing context with setting, and sometimes the inclusion of characters within that setting.
![Establishing Shot](comics-devices-library-pics/Establishing.png)

### 30. [Intertitle](https://comicsdevices.com/intertitle/)
Panels or spreads that introduce the title of the work.
![Intertitle](comics-devices-library-pics/Intertitle.png)

### 31. [Topper](https://comicsdevices.com/topper/)
A secondary row of panels or single panel that goes 'on top' of the main comic. They are typically removable and non-essential, and usually contain the comic's title.
![Topper](comics-devices-library-pics/Topper.png)

### 32. [Atmospheric Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/atmospheric-panel/)
 panel that doesn't contribute to the main narrative (to the point that it would be cut in a very efficient type of comic, no frills, no distractions), but is actually necessary to create atmosphere, mood, worldbuilding, and often reveals the sensibilities of the creator.
![Atmospheric Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Atmos.png)

### 33. [Spot Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/spot-panel/)
A panel that is not contained inside a framing outline; it takes on the shape of its subject only.
![Spot Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Spot.png)

### 34. [Vignette Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/vignette-panel/)
A panel that is not contained inside a framing outline; it takes on the shape of its subject and background.
![Vignette Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Vignette.png)

### 35. [In-Balloon Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/in-balloon-panel/)
A panel contained inside a speech balloon.
![In-Balloon Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Inballoon.png)

### 36. [Margin Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/margin-panel/)
A panel that occurs outside of the main set of panels at the page margins, often depicting a side-story or an aside.
![Margin Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Margin.png)

### 37. [Graphic Allusion and Pastiche](https://comicsdevices.com/graphic-allusion-pastiche/)
Allusion is reference to other visual, non-comics mediums (i.e paintings, illuminated manuscripts, decorative arts), artistic movements, styles or visual motifs. Pastiche is sustained imitation by intent.
![Graphic Allusion and Pastiche](comics-devices-library-pics/Allusion.png)

### 38. [Frame Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/frame-panel/)
A panel designed to frame another.
![Frame Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Frame.png)

### 39. [Catalog](https://comicsdevices.com/catalog/)
A panel or composition that lays out objects, often accompanied by names and/or description, in a fashion similar to an item catalog.
![Catalog](comics-devices-library-pics/Catalog.png)

### 40. [Diagrammatic Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/diagrammatic-panel/)
Panel in the style of a diagram or schematic.
![Diagrammatic Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Diagram.png)

### 41. [Map Panel](https://comicsdevices.com/map-panel/)
Panel done in the style of a map. Can be used as a form of exposition, defining the world, establish a new location, establish a mood, or force the reader to reorient themselves in the narrative.
![Map Panel](comics-devices-library-pics/Map.png)

### 42. [Model Composition](https://comicsdevices.com/model-composition/)
An arrangement of panels and gutters that structurally constructs and represents an object, internal or external.
![Model Composition](comics-devices-library-pics/Model.png)

### 43. [Inter-iconic Spaces](https://comicsdevices.com/inter-iconic-spaces/)
An arrangement of panels within a container that do not recreate the internal or external structure of the container.
![Inter-iconic Spaces](comics-devices-library-pics/Intericon.png)

### 44. [Unified Architectonic](https://comicsdevices.com/unified-architectonic/)
Usage of a perspective grid to structure panels.
![Unified Architectonic](comics-devices-library-pics/UnifiedArchi.png)

### 45. [Transitional Curvature](https://comicsdevices.com/transitional-curvature/)
An arrangement of panels which forms a ribbon path that twists to create perspective shifts in the narrative.
![Transitional Curvature](comics-devices-library-pics/Transition.png)

### 46. [Geometric Composition](https://comicsdevices.com/geometric-composition/)
An arrangement of panels and gutters that produces a geometric composition.
![Geometric Composition](comics-devices-library-pics/Geo.png)

### 47. [Motley Layout](https://comicsdevices.com/motley-layout/)
A sudden switch in orientation while reading.
![Motley Layout](comics-devices-library-pics/Motley.png)

### 48. [Metapanel](https://comicsdevices.com/metapanel/)
A character interacts with panels and gutters in a meta way.
![Metapanel](comics-devices-library-pics/Meta.png)

## Pacing, Colour and Symbols

### 49. [Caesura and Enjambment](https://comicsdevices.com/caesura-and-enjambment/)
Caesura is breaks or pauses between text in speech balloons. Enjambment is text that runs over from one speech balloon to the next.
![Caesura and Enjambment](comics-devices-library-pics/CaeEnjam.png)

### 50. [Ellipsis](https://comicsdevices.com/ellipsis/)
A silent, strictly visual, standalone, single panel that functions as a grammatical ellipsis (... ) would.
![Ellipsis](comics-devices-library-pics/Ellipses.png)

### 51. [Page/Screen Flip](https://comicsdevices.com/page-screen-flip/)
Utilising the formal and physical qualities of page-turning or screen-flipping for exposition, time and setting changes, dramatic effect, pacing, suspense or humour.
![Page/Screen Flip](comics-devices-library-pics/PageFlip.png)

### 52. [Colour Pop and Fade](https://comicsdevices.com/colour-pop-and-fade/)
The use of colour to control focus.
![Colour Pop and Fade](comics-devices-library-pics/ColourPopFade.png)

### 53. [Leitmotif](https://comicsdevices.com/leitmotif/)
A recurring image, symbol, visual motif or composition across the entirety of the comic, carrying with it a a specific association (to a character, time, place, memory). The meaning may dynamically transform or remain stable as the story progresses.
![Leitmotif](comics-devices-library-pics/Leitmotif.png)

### 54. [Visual Morphemes or Emanata](https://comicsdevices.com/visual-morphemes-or-emanata/)
Non-diegetic pictorial symbols representing the state of a character, subject or object from which the morphene or symbol emanates.
![Visual Morphemes or Emanata](comics-devices-library-pics/Morpheme.png)

## Balloons and Lettering

### 55. [Balloons and Lettering](https://comicsdevices.com/balloons-and-lettering/)
Balloons and lettering convey textual information and evocative elements, and control rhythm, pacing and flow.
![Balloons and Lettering](comics-devices-library-pics/BubLet.png)

### 56. [Balloon Distance](https://comicsdevices.com/balloon-distance/)
Distance between bubbles indicate length of pause.
![Balloon Distance](comics-devices-library-pics/BubDist.png)

### 57. [Evocative Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/evocative-balloon/)
A speech balloon that employs evocation via its shape and flow.
![Evocative Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/EvoBub.png)

### 58. [Corporeal Balloon & Lettering](https://comicsdevices.com/corporeal-balloon/)
Speech balloons and lettering possessing a corporeal form, physically interacting with and exerting influence on characters and objects.
![Corporeal Balloon & Lettering](comics-devices-library-pics/CorBub.png)

### 59. [Opacity Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/opacity-balloon/)
A speech ballon evoking volume and/or distance of the speaker. The lower the opacity of the bubble, the quieter and/or further away the speaker is.
![Opacity Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/Opacity.png)

### 60. [Overlap Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/overlap-balloon/)
A speech balloon that overlaps with another, but unlike the interrupting balloon, does not obscure the text of the speakers.
![Overlap Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/OverlapBub.png)

### 61. [Interrupting Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/interrupting-balloon/)
A speech balloon which interrupts another. The interruptor's speech bubble fully or partially obscures the speech of the interrupted.
![Interrupting Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/InterBub.png)

### 62. [Cut Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/cut-balloon/)
A balloon that is cut off by the edge of a panel, an object or character.
![Cut Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/CutBub.png)

### 63. [Run-On Lettering](https://comicsdevices.com/run-on-lettering/)
Lettering that runs-on, often past the edges of the balloon or panel.
![Run-On Lettering](comics-devices-library-pics/RunOn.png)

### 64. [Colour-coded Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/colour-coded-balloon/)
A speech balloon that utilises colour-coding for narrative effect; to indicate code-switching, mood, tone and character.
![Colour-coded Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/Colourcode.png)

### 65. [Tell-Tale Balloon](https://comicsdevices.com/tell-tale-balloon/)
A speech balloon drawn in the shape of an object or being.
![Tell-Tale Balloon](comics-devices-library-pics/TellTale.png)

### 66. [Pictorial Lettering](https://comicsdevices.com/pictorial-lettering/)
Lettering that uses symbols or iconography to express dialogue.
![Pictorial Lettering](comics-devices-library-pics/PicLet.png)

### 67. [Balloon tag](https://comicsdevices.com/balloon-tag/)
Icons (Tags) used to connect a disembodied balloon to a speaker.
![Balloon tag](comics-devices-library-pics/BalloonTag.png)

### 68. [Manicule](https://comicsdevices.com/manicule/)
A pointer, usually a hand, emanating from or pointing towards a caption box that serves to highlight part of the narrative or add to it, although it is not literally part of the narrative.
![Manicule](comics-devices-library-pics/Manicule.png)

### 69. [Code Switch](https://comicsdevices.com/code-switch/)
The utilisation of different speech balloons, lettering styles, symbols or mark-making to represent characters speaking a different language.
![Code Switch](comics-devices-library-pics/Codeswitch.png)

### 70. [Aside](https://comicsdevices.com/aside/)
A short comment that sits outside of a balloon or character that is not perceived by anyone except the comment maker and the reader. An aside may come from the author, usually placed outside of the panel or near the edges.
![Aside](comics-devices-library-pics/Aside.png)
